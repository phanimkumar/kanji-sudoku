Orbit Launcher

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Orbit Launcher ("Orbit"), developed by Swayamkrushi, handles information when you use the Orbit Android application.

Privacy at a glance: Orbit is designed to function primarily on your device. Orbit does not require an account and does not collect, sell, or share personal information.

1. About Orbit

Orbit is an Android home-screen launcher that provides an alternative way to view, navigate, and launch applications installed on your device.

2. Information About Installed Applications

To function as a launcher, Orbit accesses information about applications that are installed and available for launching on your device.

This may include information such as:

  • Application name
  • Application icon
  • Application package information required for launching the app

This information is used to display and launch applications through the Orbit interface.

Information about your installed applications is processed locally on your device and is not transmitted by Orbit to Swayamkrushi or stored on our servers.

3. Personal Information

Orbit does not require you to create an account, register, or provide personal information in order to use the application.

The current version of Orbit does not collect or transmit:

  • Contacts
  • Call logs
  • SMS or text messages
  • Photos or videos
  • Personal files
  • Precise location
  • Microphone recordings
  • Camera data

4. Data Collection and Sharing

Orbit does not collect or store personal information on external servers.

Orbit does not sell, rent, or share personal information with advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties.

Orbit also does not transmit your installed application list to Swayamkrushi.

5. Local Device Processing

Core launcher functions are performed locally on your Android device. Information required to display and launch applications remains on the device.

6. Third-Party Applications

Orbit allows you to launch third-party applications installed on your device.

Once another application is opened, its operation and data practices are governed by that application's own terms and privacy policy. Swayamkrushi is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party applications.

7. Children's Privacy

Orbit does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Because Orbit does not require an account and does not collect personal information through its core launcher functionality, no personal profile is created for users, including children.

8. Security

Orbit is designed to minimize data collection. Information required for its core launcher functionality is processed locally on the user's device.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Orbit may receive additional features in future versions. If a future feature changes the way information is collected, processed, transmitted, or shared, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

The effective date shown at the top of this page will be updated whenever material changes are made.
