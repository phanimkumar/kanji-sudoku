Privacy Policy – SpendLens

Last updated: 23 August 2026

SpendLens is a personal expense and transaction tracking application designed to help users understand financial transactions from SMS messages available on their device.

SMS Access

SpendLens requests permission to access SMS messages solely to identify financial transaction notifications such as debits, credits, payments and transfers received from banks and payment services.

SMS messages are processed locally on the user's device. SpendLens does not upload SMS messages to our servers.

Information Stored by SpendLens

SpendLens extracts transaction-related information required for its features, such as transaction amount, date, transaction type, account reference and beneficiary or sender information where available.

Extracted transaction information is stored locally in the application's database on the user's device.

SpendLens does not store copies of the user's original SMS messages in its application database.

Data Transmission

SpendLens does not transmit, upload or synchronize the user's SMS messages or extracted financial transaction records to our servers.

The application currently operates using data stored locally on the user's device.

Reports and Exports

SpendLens allows users to generate and export transaction reports, including Excel and PDF reports. These exports are created at the user's request and handled through the user's device and the applications or locations selected by the user for saving or sharing them.

Data Sharing

SpendLens does not sell users' personal or financial information.

SpendLens does not share SMS messages or extracted transaction information with advertisers or other third parties.

Data Retention and Deletion

Extracted transaction information remains stored locally on the user's device. Users can remove locally stored application data by clearing the application's data or uninstalling SpendLens.

Security

SpendLens is designed to minimize collection and transmission of personal information by processing financial SMS messages and maintaining extracted transaction information locally on the user's device.

Children

SpendLens is a personal financial utility and is not designed or directed specifically toward children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when SpendLens features or data-handling practices change. Any updated version will be published at this Privacy Policy location.
