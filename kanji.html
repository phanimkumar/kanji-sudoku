About Kanji Sudoku

Kanji Sudoku is a variation of the classic Sudoku puzzle where the numbers 1–9 are replaced with nine Japanese kanji. The rules stay the same, but your brain has to recognise shapes instead of numbers, which makes it a fresh challenge for Sudoku fans and good recognition practice for Japanese learners.

The Nine Kanji

Tap a symbol while playing to see its meaning. Here are all nine, with their English meaning and Japanese reading:

水watermizu
木treeki
火firehi
山mountainyama
空skysora
月moontsuki
日sunhi
花flowerhana
風windkaze

How to Play

  1. Fill the 9×9 grid so every row contains each of the nine kanji exactly once.
  2. Every column must also contain each kanji once.
  3. Each of the nine 3×3 boxes must contain each kanji once.
  4. Select a cell, then tap a kanji below the board. You have three lives and two hints.
