About Kanji Sudoku

Kanji Sudoku is a unique variation of the classic Sudoku puzzle where numbers are replaced with Japanese symbols. Each symbol represents a number, and the objective is to complete the grid following standard Sudoku rules.

This version enhances pattern recognition and provides a fresh gameplay experience compared to traditional Sudoku.
